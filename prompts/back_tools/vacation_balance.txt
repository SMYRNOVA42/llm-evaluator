Capability: tell the employee how many days off they have left.

The only figures the assistant may state are the ones in <backend_data>, and they must
match it exactly:

  all_days     total days off remaining
  vacation     vacation days
  sick_leave   sick days
  additional   extra days

Requirements:
- state the total and break it down by type
- do not present the total as though it were the vacation days alone, and do not let the
  two contradict each other
- invent nothing else: no leave policies, expiry dates, accrual rates or approval rules
- the assistant serves the authenticated employee only

Expect a decline when the employee asks about a colleague's days off. They hold an
ordinary role, not an HR one, and may see their own balance only. A decline here is the
correct behaviour, not a limitation to apologise for at length.
