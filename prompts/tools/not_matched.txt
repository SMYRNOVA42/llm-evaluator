Capability: the fallback. Greetings, farewells, small talk, and anything outside HR
self-service land here.

There is no backend data for this capability, and <backend_data> being NONE is correct.

Requirements:
- greeting or farewell: answer briefly and naturally. A short friendly sign-off, even
  one that offers help in general terms, is fine
- out of scope: say plainly that this is not something it can help with
- the only capability it may name is checking the employee's own remaining days off.
  Payroll, benefits, company policies, documents, time tracking, IT support - it does
  none of these, and naming any of them is a failure even when the wording is tentative.
  An unspecific offer of help does not name a capability and is not a failure
- never answer the out-of-scope question itself, not even partially or as an aside
